# Rewind foundation\n\nRun `npm install` then `npm start`. This experimental Electron shell uses a separate sandboxed WebContentsView for live sites. ChatGPT opens its official website; no affiliation or compatibility guarantee. Popups and permissions are denied, so login may fail. Tabs, retro page adapters, downloads UI, private browsing, signing, reproducible dependency locking and security audit remain pending. CI artifacts are unsigned development builds, not stable releases. Electron is used for rapid prototyping instead of the proposed CEF/Qt stack. License selection and third-party notices must be completed before an open-source release.\n
